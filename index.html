The street network is today's (OpenStreetMap), not 1920's: some streets did not exist yet, and some were renamed or rerouted. Public transport, slope and lighting are not included. Only the 1920 listings that could be matched to a modern address appear (about three in four); private nurses, dressmakers, music teachers and ministers, who worked from home, are left out.
